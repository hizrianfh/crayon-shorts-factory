In nineteen oh four, the Olympic marathon was won by a man who rode in a car.
Saint Louis, Missouri. Over ninety degrees, dusty roads, one water stop.
Fred Lorz got cramps at mile nine. He climbed into his manager's car, and rode for eleven miles, waving at runners.
When the car broke down, he jogged into the stadium. First place! The crowd roared. The President's daughter was ready to crown him.
Then someone spotted the car. Banned for life.
But the real winner, Thomas Hicks, wasn't clean either.
His trainers kept him running on brandy, egg whites, and rat poison.
And back then, there was no rule against it.
